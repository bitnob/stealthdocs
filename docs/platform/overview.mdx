The Bitnob Platform API provides **non-custodial**, policy-governed wallets for banks, exchanges, and fintech companies. You hold the assets. Private keys are generated inside hardware-isolated enclaves, every signature is governed by policies you write, and Bitnob cannot move funds on your behalf.

<Note>
  **The Platform API is a separate product from the Bitnob Business API**, with its own keys, base URL, and authentication. [Choose a product](/docs/choose-a-product) compares the two.
</Note>

## What non-custodial means here

Three properties define it, and each is enforced in hardware rather than by policy alone.

- **Keys never leave the enclave.** They are generated inside AWS Nitro Enclaves and are never exposed, including to Bitnob engineers.
- **Every signature passes your policies first.** The enclave releases a signature only when every active rule permits it.
- **There is no administrative override.** Bitnob cannot sign a transaction for you. Root quorum governance applies even to Bitnob-side operations on your organisation.

## What the platform gives you

Managing private keys, signing transactions, enforcing spending limits, and coordinating approvals across several blockchains is hard engineering. This platform provides:

- Hardware-isolated key management that no single person on your team can bypass
- A declarative policy engine that runs before every signing event
- Multi-chain support across EVM chains, Bitcoin, Solana, and Tron
- Gas sponsorship, so your customers never hold a fee token
- Batch transfers and HD wallets that scale from one customer to millions

Signing latency is 50 to 100 ms, and there are no volume caps.

## Key features

- **Bitnob Secure Enclave.** Private keys are generated and stored inside AWS Nitro Enclaves, hardware-isolated memory that never exposes key material, not even to Bitnob engineers. Every signing event is logged as an immutable audit trail.
- **Declarative policy engine.** Set spending limits, address whitelists, velocity caps, time locks, and multi-signature approval thresholds as declarative rules. Policies run before every signing event. The Enclave only releases a signature when every rule passes.
- **HD vault hierarchy.** A single BIP-39 seed generates unlimited wallets and addresses across all supported chains. Use a dedicated [vault](/api-reference/platform/vaults/list-vaults) for treasury wallets or a pool vault to assign one [wallet](/api-reference/platform/wallets/list-wallets) per customer.
- **Gas station abstraction.** Pre-fund gas stations once per chain and let Bitnob sponsor fees automatically on every outbound transaction. On Tron, stake TRX for energy and bandwidth to minimise costs further.
- **50–100 ms signing latency.** The Bitnob Secure Enclave is optimised for low-latency signing. Fully governed transactions are signed and broadcast within milliseconds of request.

## Common use cases

- A crypto exchange that needs [one deposit address per customer per chain](/api-reference/platform/wallets/create-address), with automated withdrawal governance: see the [Exchange integration guide](/docs/platform/exchange-integration-guide)
- A neo-bank that holds customer balances in Bitcoin and stablecoins and needs to enforce daily spend caps
- A treasury management product that rebalances across chains and requires dual-admin approval for large transfers
- A fintech platform that processes cross-border payments and must comply with address-whitelist requirements
- A DeFi aggregator that [sponsors gas fees](/api-reference/platform/gas-stations/sponsor-and-broadcast-external-tron-transaction) for its users using USDC and ETH across multiple L2s

## Designed for institutions

Platform is built with:

- **Non-custodial by design**: keys never leave the hardware boundary; Bitnob cannot move funds unilaterally
- **Root quorum governance**: a configurable set of senior approvers must sign off on policy changes themselves
- **Immutable audit logs** for every signing event, policy change, and approval decision
- **Per-day RPC quotas** on managed blockchain nodes so you don't need to run your own infrastructure
- **Idempotency keys** on all mutation endpoints, such as [Create Transaction](/api-reference/platform/transactions/create-transaction), to guarantee safe retries under network failures

You focus on the product experience. We handle key management, signing infrastructure, policy enforcement, and chain connectivity.
