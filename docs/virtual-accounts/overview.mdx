A virtual account is a dedicated Nigerian bank account number assigned to one of your customers. Anyone can pay into it by ordinary bank transfer. Bitnob identifies the deposit and credits **your company's** [NGN balance](/api-reference/balances/list-balances), tagged with the account it came through, so you always know which customer paid.

It's the simplest way to receive naira: give each customer their own account number and stop matching transfers to people by hand.

## When to use it

| You are building | Virtual accounts let you |
|---|---|
| A wallet or fintech app | Give every user a personal account number to top up from any Nigerian bank. |
| Invoicing or collections | Issue an account per customer, so every payment arrives already attributed. |
| A crypto on-ramp | Collect naira, then convert it with an [NGN trade](/docs/trading/overview). |

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set up the customer">
    Every virtual account belongs to a customer, and Nigerian regulation requires a verified identity behind it. The [customer record](/api-reference/customers/create-customer) must carry a BVN, date of birth, and contact details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Issue the account">
    One call to [Create virtual account](/api-reference/virtual-accounts/create-virtual-account) returns the account number and bank name to show your customer. Choose the issuing bank with `bank`, or leave it out to use the default.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your customer pays in">
    The customer sends a bank transfer to the account number. Bitnob credits your NGN balance and tags the deposit with the virtual account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You track the deposit">
    Each deposit fires a `virtual_account.deposit.success` webhook. [Get account transactions](/api-reference/virtual-accounts/get-account-transactions) lists everything an account has received.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The collection model

| Topic | Detail |
|---|---|
| Currency | NGN only |
| Issuing banks | Nombank MFB (`nomba`, the default) or Globus Bank (`globus`) |
| Where funds land | Your company NGN balance, not a per-customer balance |
| Accounts per customer | As many as you need, including one at each bank |
| Collection fee | None |
| Funding limits | No per-deposit, daily, or monthly limit |
| Crediting time | Within 10 minutes of the transfer, barring delays at the sending bank |
| Amounts | Kobo, as an integer string: `"100000"` is ₦1,000.00 |

## Getting access

The Bitnob team enables virtual accounts and NGN trading together once your KYB is complete. There's no self-serve toggle, and review usually takes 24 to 48 hours after all your documents are in. Until then, virtual account calls in production are rejected.
