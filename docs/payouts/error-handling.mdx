A payout crosses a blockchain, an FX conversion, a compliance check, and a local banking rail. Each can fail independently, and each failure surfaces differently: some as API errors, some as webhook events, and some only in the record's status. This page covers every case.

Two rules underpin all of them:

- Track by the payout `id` and your `reference`; the [Trip timeline](/docs/payouts/understanding-trip-timeline) tells you exactly which stage a payout stalled at.
- Failure emits no webhook. Only `payouts.withdrawal.success` and `payouts.withdrawal.expired` are terminal events; a `FAILED` record is found by polling [Get payout](/api-reference/payouts/get-payout). Build a reconciliation sweep, not just a webhook handler.

## Failure scenarios

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Expired quote">
    The quote passed `expires_at` before you initialized, or before funding arrived.

    **Detection:** using an expired `quoteId` returns `410 QUOTE_EXPIRED`. An initialized but unfunded payout emits `payouts.withdrawal.expired`.

    **Response:** request a fresh quote and start over. Nothing was settled. In your UI, check `expires_at` before showing payment instructions, and stop showing them once it passes.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Underpayment">
    The funding payment arrived short of the quoted amount.

    **Behaviour:** the payout still processes; the fiat amount delivered is adjusted down proportionally at the quoted rate.

    **Response:** read the delivered amount from the payout record rather than assuming the quoted amount, and tell the recipient-facing side of your product that an adjusted amount was delivered.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Overpayment">
    The funding payment exceeded the quoted amount.

    **Behaviour:** the intended fiat amount is paid out; the excess is credited to your Bitnob balance.

    **Response:** nothing required for delivery. Track the excess in your reconciliation so your books match your balance.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Payment after expiry">
    Funds arrived on-chain after the quote window closed.

    **Behaviour:** the payout does not proceed automatically. Depending on configuration the amount is refunded, credited to your balance, or held for manual resolution with support.

    **Response:** design your payment screen so paying late is hard: show a countdown from `expires_at` and hide the address once it passes.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Funding stuck on the blockchain">
    The deposit was sent with too low a fee and confirms slowly.

    **Detection:** the Trip shows `asset_received_at` set but `asset_confirmed_at` still null.

    **Behaviour:** the payout waits; nothing proceeds until confirmation thresholds are met.

    **Response:** show an "awaiting confirmations" state. For Bitcoin funding, recommend a competitive fee; for Lightning, this case does not exist, which is a reason to prefer it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Delivery failure">
    Funding succeeded, but the bank or mobile money operator rejected the payout: an invalid account, a closed account, or rail downtime.

    **Detection:** the record moves to `FAILED`. No webhook fires; catch it with your reconciliation sweep against [Get payout](/api-reference/payouts/get-payout) or [List payouts](/api-reference/payouts/list-payouts).

    **Response:** read the failure reason from the record, correct the beneficiary if that was the cause, re-run [Account lookup](/api-reference/payouts/account-lookup), and retry with a fresh quote. [Validate a beneficiary](/docs/payouts/validate-a-beneficiary) before initializing prevents most of this class outright.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Compliance rejection">
    The payout was blocked after funding by a compliance or risk check: a flagged recipient or a country restriction.

    **Detection:** same as delivery failure, the record moves to `FAILED`.

    **Response:** this is not retryable by changing details. Route it to your operations team, and engage your Bitnob account manager to resolve or refund.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Your webhook endpoint is down">
    Not a payout failure, but it looks like one from inside your system: payouts complete and your records never hear about it.

    **Behaviour:** Bitnob retries failed deliveries 3 times after the first failure.

    **Response:** idempotent handlers, fast `200`s, and the reconciliation sweep as the backstop. See [Receive payout webhooks](/docs/payouts/webhook-payout).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## The reconciliation sweep

The single mechanism that catches everything above: on a schedule, list recent payouts and compare terminal states against your records.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sweep">
    Query [List payouts](/api-reference/payouts/list-payouts) for your open references.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Settle">
    Mark `COMPLETED` records delivered, exactly as if the success webhook had arrived.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Route failures">
    Send `FAILED` records to your retry or operations path.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Alert on stalls">
    Flag payouts that have sat in one stage beyond your corridor's expected window; per-corridor expectations are in [Corridor behaviour](/docs/payouts/corridor-specific-behavior).
  </Step>
</Steps>
