---
title: "Troubleshooting: payout stuck in pending"
description: "Why a payout sits in pending or processing, how to tell a funding delay from a real failure, and what to check before contacting support."
---

A payout that stays in `pending` is almost always waiting on one thing: funding. This page walks through what each status actually means, where payouts commonly stall, and how to confirm final state instead of guessing.

## What the statuses mean

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| pending | Created and awaiting funding. |
| processing | Funded; settlement to the beneficiary is in progress. |
| completed | Funds delivered to the beneficiary. |
| failed | The payout could not be completed. |

`pending` is not an error state. It is the normal resting state of a payout that has been created but not yet funded.

## Cause 1: the payout was never funded

For **offchain** payouts, your account balance is charged at finalize. If finalize succeeded, the payout proceeds automatically and should move to `processing` quickly.

For **onchain** payouts, nothing happens until your crypto deposit arrives at the address returned during initialization. The payout waits in `pending` until:

- the deposit confirms on-chain (BTC), or
- the Lightning payment settles, or
- the stablecoin deposit confirms.

**Check:** in sandbox you cannot broadcast a real deposit. Simulate it with `POST /api/payouts/quotes/{quoteId}/deposits` and a body of `{ "amount": "50000" }`. A sandbox onchain payout left without a simulated deposit will sit in `pending` forever, which is expected behavior, not a bug.

## Cause 2: you are reading the wrong status field

Webhook payloads and status lookups do not always use the same vocabulary for the same state. If your code compares a webhook's `data.status` value against the values documented for Get Payout, a valid transition can look like "no change" to your system even though the payout moved forward.

**Check:** log the raw webhook body and the raw response from `GET /api/payouts/{id}` side by side for the same payout. Map both vocabularies explicitly in your integration rather than assuming they match.

## Cause 3: it is genuinely still processing

Settlement to banks and mobile-money operators is not instant and varies by corridor. A payout in `processing` is funded and moving; there is nothing to retry.

**Check:** poll `GET /api/payouts/{id}` or rely on webhooks at your `callback_url`. Treat `completed` as the only success state and confirm it via lookup or webhook rather than assuming success from the finalize response.

## Escalating to support

If a payout has been in `pending` well past your deposit's confirmation, or in `processing` far longer than the corridor's normal settlement time, contact support with:

- the payout `reference` you set at initialization,
- the `correlation_id` from the relevant API response,
- the quote ID and, for onchain payouts, the deposit transaction hash.
