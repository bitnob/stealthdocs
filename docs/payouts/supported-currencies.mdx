## Currencies and rails

  This table is the set of corridors with live payouts. [Supported countries](/api-reference/payouts/supported-countries) can list corridors that aren't live yet, so confirm a new corridor with your account manager before you launch it.
</Note>

## What each rail means for speed

The rail, not Bitnob, sets how long the fiat leg takes. Size your SLAs around the slowest rail you use.

| Rail | Typical speed |
|---|---|
| NIP (Nigeria) | Seconds. Real-time interbank settlement via NIBSS. |
| Mobile money | Seconds to minutes. The only rail in Kenya, The Gambia, Rwanda, Uganda, and the CFA franc countries listed above, and one of two in Ghana. |
| Local bank transfer (USD) | Varies by country. |
| ACH (US domestic) | One to three business days. Cheaper than wire. |
| Wire and SWIFT | Same day to several business days. Used for high-value and urgent cross-border payouts. |

Corridors differ in more than speed. See [Corridor behaviour](/docs/payouts/corridor-specific-behavior) for the operational detail per country.

## Requesting a new corridor

New currencies and channels are added based on demand and available liquidity. If you need a destination that is not listed, contact your account manager or the [support centre](https://support.bitnob.com).
