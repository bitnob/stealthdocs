Bitnob is a financial infrastructure API. It gives you one integration for bitcoin, stablecoins, and local currency rails across Africa, so you can move money between them without holding accounts or licences in each market.

Every request goes to `https://api.bitnob.com` and is signed with HMAC. Two pages take you from nothing to a working call: get credentials, then sign your first request.

## Two products, two custody models

Everything above is the **Bitnob API**: Bitnob holds the balance and settles on your behalf. That is the right choice for payouts, cards, trading, and virtual accounts.

If you need to hold the assets yourself, that is a different product.

| | Bitnob API | Platform |
|---|---|---|
| Who holds the funds | Bitnob | You |
| Built for | Payouts, cards, trading, virtual accounts | Wallets, addresses, and governed signing across chains |
| Base URL | `https://api.bitnob.com` | `https://api.platform.bitnob.com` |
| Authentication | HMAC request signing | `X-API-Key` header |

<Card title="Platform" icon="wallet" href="/docs/platform/overview">
  Non-custodial, policy-governed wallets. Keys are generated in hardware enclaves and Bitnob cannot move funds on your behalf.
</Card>

## A typical integration

Most products follow the same shape, so learning one carries over to the rest.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a customer">
    Most balances and payment objects belong to a customer, not to your account directly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fund a balance">
    Receive bitcoin or stablecoins to an issued address, or take NGN through a virtual account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Quote, then execute">
    Conversions and payouts are quoted first. A quote is only valid for a limited window.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm with a webhook">
    Money movement settles asynchronously. Treat the webhook as the source of truth, not the API response.
  </Step>
</Steps>
