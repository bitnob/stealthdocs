The card issuing API creates USD-denominated virtual Visa cards on demand, backed by regulated card issuers. Cards are prepaid: you fund them from your Bitnob company balance, and the cardholder spends online anywhere Visa is accepted, or in store via Apple or Google Wallet tap-to-pay.

Bitnob is the technology and distribution layer. Licensed issuing banks and Visa programme managers handle issuance, onboarding, and merchant settlement, so you integrate one API without managing Visa relationships or card compliance yourself.

![Virtual card](/images/virtualCard.svg)

## When to use it

| You are building | Cards let you |
|---|---|
| A fintech or crypto app | Let users spend wallet or stablecoin balance anywhere Visa works. |
| Payroll or remittance | Deliver spendable cards instead of bank deposits. |
| Corporate spend control | Issue per-employee or per-department cards, fund each with only what it needs, and cap it with [spending limits](/docs/card-issuing/spending-limits). |
| Programmatic disbursement | Auto-issue a funded card per payout, campaign, or event. |

Cards are online-first and restricted by design: no ATM withdrawals, USD only, and no 3DS yet. That trade keeps fraud low and issuance instant.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Verify the cardholder">
    Submit the cardholder to [Card KYC](/api-reference/cards/card-kyc). Approval returns the `customer_id` every card for that person is issued against. With a BVN, approval can come back in the same response.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the card">
    Call [Create card](/api-reference/cards/create-card) with the `customer_id` and the opening `amount`. The card comes back `pending` and goes `active` once the issuer provisions it, usually within seconds. [Get card](/api-reference/cards/get-card) then shows the last four digits; the full card details come from [Get secure card details](/api-reference/cards/get-secure-card-details).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Top it up">
    Add value later with [Fund or withdraw balance](/api-reference/cards/fund-or-withdraw-balance).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Spend and monitor">
    The cardholder pays online or via tap-to-pay. Every authorisation, settlement, refund, and decline reaches you as a [webhook event](/docs/card-issuing/webhooks) and in the [transaction history](/api-reference/cards/list-card-transactions-by-card).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Control the lifecycle">
    [Freeze or unfreeze](/api-reference/cards/freeze-or-unfreeze) in real time, and [terminate](/api-reference/cards/terminate-card) permanently.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The funding model

| Topic | Detail |
|---|---|
| Currency | USD only |
| Funded from | Your company balance. If it can't cover a card, the error's `extensions.currency` names the balance that was checked. |
| Balance model | Prepaid; no overdraft or credit |
| Amounts | Integers in micro-units: 1 USD = 1,000,000 |

Creating a card costs the opening `amount` plus a \$1.00 creation fee plus the top-up fee on that amount (\$1.00 below \$100). A \$2.00 card takes \$4.00 from your balance. Every fee is on [Fees and decline rules](/docs/card-issuing/card-fees).
