Issuing a card is one API call, but the card is created at a regulated issuing bank, not inside Bitnob. Understanding that hop explains everything about how issuance behaves: why a card starts `pending`, why the webhook is the source of truth, and why customer identity details are required up front.

## The issuance flow

```mermaid

    B --> C[Issuer creates the card]
    C --> D[virtualcard.created.completed<br/>status: active]
    C --> E[virtualcard.created.failed]
```

Your [Create card](/api-reference/cards/create-card) call returns immediately with the card in status `pending` and `created_status: processing`. The issuer then provisions the card, usually within seconds. `virtualcard.created.completed` confirms it is `active`; only then are the PAN, CVV, and expiry retrievable via [Get secure card details](/api-reference/cards/get-secure-card-details).

Treat the webhook, not the HTTP response, as the signal to show the card to a user.

## Verify the cardholder first

Every card belongs to a verified customer, and the issuing bank requires that customer's identity: name, date of birth, ID document, and address. Run each cardholder through [Card KYC](/api-reference/cards/card-kyc) before you create a card for them. Approval returns a `customer_id`, which you pass to Create card. Because the identity details were captured during KYC, you don't send them again.

Cards are virtual only. Lite cards, created with [Create lite card](/api-reference/cards/create-lite-card), also need approved Card KYC, plus `card_brand`, `swipe_count`, and `terminate_date`.

Higher volumes or limits can require additional cardholder verification.

## What creating a card costs

Create card takes the opening `amount` from your company balance, plus two fees: the \$1.00 card creation fee, and the top-up fee on the opening amount (\$1.00 flat below \$100, 1% at \$100 and above). They show up as separate ledger entries: a `CARD_TOPUP` carrying the top-up fee, and a `CARD_CREATION_FEES` debit. A \$2.00 card therefore needs \$4.00.

If your balance can't cover all three, Create card fails before anything is issued:

```json
{
  "type": "https://api.bitnob.com/errors/VALIDATION_ERROR",
  "title": "Validation Error",
  "status": 400,
  "detail": "Insufficient company balance for card creation: required 4.00, available 0.11 USDC",
  "code": "VALIDATION_ERROR",
  "extensions": {
    "error_code": "COMPANY_INSUFFICIENT_BALANCE",
    "currency": "USDC",
    "required": "4.00",
    "available": "0.11"
  }
}
```

`extensions.currency` tells you which balance was checked, and `required` is the full amount including fees. Top that balance up, then retry.

## What kind of card you get

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Scheme | Visa |
| Form | Virtual, delivered instantly |
| Currency | USD |
| Spend scope | Online (card-not-present), plus in-store tap-to-pay via Apple or Google Wallet when contactless is enabled |
| 3DS | Not currently supported |
| Cards per customer | Up to 3 active cards |
| Billing address | A fixed US billing address issued with the card and returned in `billing_address` |

## When to issue

| Scenario | Pattern |
|---|---|
| User wants to shop online | One general-purpose card linked to their account, reused across merchants. |
| Subscriptions | A persistent card left active for recurring charges. |
| Business spend | Per-employee or per-department cards, each funded with only what that person needs. |
| One-time purchase | A disposable card, terminated after use. |
| Payout-to-spend | Issue and fund a card at payout time instead of transferring fiat. |

Creating a card is not idempotent by customer: calling create twice makes two cards. Track active cards per customer in your own system, or check with [Get cards by customer](/api-reference/cards/get-cards-by-customer) before issuing.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How long does creation take?">
    Usually seconds. The card is `pending` until the issuer confirms; `virtualcard.created.completed` is the activation signal.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can a user have multiple cards?">
    Yes, up to 3 active cards per customer at any time.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are cards reusable?">
    Yes, until frozen, terminated, or expired. See [Freeze, unfreeze, or terminate a card](/docs/card-issuing/card-status-management).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does creation cost?">
    A one-time fee per card, charged whether or not the card is ever funded. See [Fees and decline rules](/docs/card-issuing/card-fees).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
