The trading API converts between USDT, USDC, Bitcoin, and Nigerian naira in two calls: [create a quote](/api-reference/trading/create-quote), then [submit an order](/api-reference/trading/submit-order). Pricing, liquidity routing across exchanges and OTC desks, execution, and settlement are handled behind the API.

Execution is atomic: an order fills completely or not at all, so there are no partial fills and no slippage drift. Settled assets land directly in the matching Bitnob balance on either side of the trade, and there are no daily or monthly volume caps. Crypto pairs have a maximum size per quote, and NGN pairs are limited to ₦500 to ₦10,000,000 per trade.

![Trading](/images/trading.svg)

## Supported pairs

| Pair | Direction |
|---|---|
| USDT ↔ BTC | Either way |
| USDC ↔ BTC | Either way |
| USDC ↔ USDT | Either way |
| NGN ↔ USDT | Either way |
| NGN ↔ USDC | Either way |
| NGN ↔ BTC | Either way |

Live rates for the crypto pairs (BTC/USDT, BTC/USDC, USDC/USDT) come from [List all prices](/api-reference/trading/list-all-prices) and [Get price by pair](/api-reference/trading/get-price-by-pair). NGN pairs aren't returned there; read indicative naira rates from [Get exchange rate](/api-reference/exchange-rates/get-single-pair-rate).

<Note>
  The Bitnob team enables NGN trading together with virtual accounts once your KYB is complete. There's no self-serve toggle, and review usually takes 24 to 48 hours after all documents are in. NGN orders always need a locked quote; see [Create a quote](/docs/trading/create-quote).
</Note>

## The two-call flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Quote">
    [Create a quote](/docs/trading/create-quote) to lock a rate for a short window: about 10 seconds in production, and in sandbox 30 seconds for NGN pairs and about 5 minutes for crypto pairs. Or skip it and trade at the live market rate for crypto pairs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Order">
    [Execute the trade](/docs/trading/execute-trade) with the quote's `id` and `price`, plus your unique `reference` for idempotency.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    `trade.completed` or `trade.failed` reports the outcome; see [Receive trade webhooks](/docs/trading/trade-webhooks). Balances update immediately on completion.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Beyond spot orders, the API also supports [scheduled orders](/api-reference/trading/create-scheduled-order) on a recurring cadence and [target orders](/api-reference/trading/create-target-order) that execute when a price is reached.
