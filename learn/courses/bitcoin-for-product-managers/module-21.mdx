## 21.3 Role-specific master action plans

### Product managers

**Master tasks:**

- Design product KPIs before designing features.
- For every Bitcoin flow, answer:
  - "Is the user sovereign here?"
  - "Is risk properly explained?"
  - "Is survival possible if everything goes wrong?"
- Build launch plans that include:
  - Seed phrase backup at creation,
  - Transparent fee communication,
  - Liquidity stress tests.
- Build loyalty loops, not just referral links.
- Always answer: "Would I use this if I were a refugee?"

### Designers

**Master tasks:**

- Create onboarding flows that:
  - Force backup before first use,
  - Teach quietly, not aggressively,
  - Respect fear, low literacy, low bandwidth.
- Design for:
  - Fear: Calm tones, big buttons.
  - Flight: Offline modes, QR-based flows.
  - Confiscation risk: Hidden apps, stealth wallets.
- Simplify screens until an exhausted, scared person could still use them safely.
- Build default privacy, not optional privacy.

### Business/founders/leadership

**Master tasks:**

- Build revenue transparently:
  - Fair spreads,
  - Clear fees,
  - No "dark patterns."
- Scale with liquidity protections:
  - Hot/cold wallet discipline,
  - Operational security readiness,
  - Risk rebalancing models.
- Treat compliance as user protection, not government appeasement.
- Build Proof-of-Reserves if possible.
- Build culture that loves Bitcoin users, not just Bitcoin narratives.

## 21.4 Final self-test challenges

**Challenge 1: Survival simulation.** Assume you have 5 minutes to leave your country. Can your Bitcoin product allow you to survive across a border with nothing but memory?

✅ If no, fix your wallet backup flows immediately.

**Challenge 2: Trust audit.** Read your own app/storefront/landing page. Would an ordinary user feel educated, empowered, and respected? Or manipulated and confused?

✅ Rewrite until dignity shines through.

**Challenge 3: Circular economy design sprint.** Sketch how you would activate 10 merchants in your city to accept bitcoin in 7 days.

✅ If your plan depends on "deep education" or "technical training," you’re wrong.

✅ If it depends on payment flows, incentives, visibility, you’re right.

## 21.5 Lifetime Bitcoin product checklist

- Backup before first use.
- Self-custody pathways, not prisons.
- Liquidity stress tests quarterly.
- Transparent pricing on every screen.
- Privacy defaults, not opt-ins.
- No fiat psychology infects Bitcoin flows.
- Educate at the moment of action, not during onboarding walls.
- Build Bitcoin experiences that survive power cuts, surveillance, censorship, terror.
- Build Bitcoin experiences that lift users, not just your valuation.
- Build for 20 years, not 20 months.
- Build as if Bitcoin is your only boss.
