12 modules plus a capstone project on moving money across borders with Bitcoin and stablecoins as settlement rails.

This course is designed for product managers building payout products. It covers:

- Why moving money is different from moving data,
- Corridors, settlement models, and the payout lifecycle stage by stage,
- Error handling, user expectations, treasury basics, and security principles,
- Onboarding, KYC/KYB, compliance, and the KPIs that measure payout success.

It ends with a capstone project and a companion essay on Bitcoin and stablecoin settlement use cases.
