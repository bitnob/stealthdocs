10 modules on virtual card fraud: how it happens, how to detect it, and how to stay audit-ready when it does.

This course is written for risk, fraud, compliance, and support teams running a card product in production. It covers:

- Fraud patterns specific to virtual cards, from MCC and BIN abuse to chargeback fraud,
- Transaction pattern detection and control design,
- Incident response and post-fraud investigation workflows,
- Logging, audit trails, PCI-DSS, and scheme requirements.
