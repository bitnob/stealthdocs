23 modules on the full virtual card lifecycle: registration, issuance, funding, spending, disputes, and the economics that decide whether a card program makes money.

This course is written for product managers, founders, and operators building card products on top of an issuing API. It covers:

- The card lifecycle from a product perspective, not just an API perspective,
- Fees, interchange, cross-border charges, and revenue modeling,
- Fraud patterns, spend abuse, disputes, and chargebacks,
- UX, support operations, metrics, and go-to-market for card products.
