21 modules, one discipline: build Bitcoin products that survive, serve, and scale.

This course is designed for Product Managers who are serious about building Bitcoin-powered products that protect users, survive pressure, grow responsibly, and outlast hype cycles.

Across 21 practical, deeply human-centered modules, you will learn not just how Bitcoin works, but how to design, prioritize, and ship Bitcoin experiences that:

- Serve merchants, migrants, activists, and everyday savers,
- Protect autonomy, liquidity, privacy, and dignity,
- Sustain business models without betraying user trust,
- Build resilient, ethical companies that will stand for decades.

You will leave this journey not simply as a PM who "understands Bitcoin", but as a builder of infrastructures that defend freedom, stability, and future generations.

This is not a crypto product management course.

This is Bitcoin product discipline.

And it is built for those who intend to lead.

## Full table of contents

| Module | Title | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | What Bitcoin Really Is | Understand Bitcoin as a system of money, freedom, and survival not just digital currency and why its principles must shape product decisions. |
| 2 | Private Keys, Public Keys, and Bitcoin Addresses | Master how Bitcoin keys and addresses work, how ownership is proven, and how your product must secure them correctly. |
| 3 | Understanding UTXOs | Learn why Bitcoin is not account-based, how UTXOs represent real spendable Bitcoin, and how they affect product design and fees. |
| 4 | Anatomy of a Bitcoin Transaction | Break down the structure of Bitcoin transactions: inputs, outputs, signatures, scripts and design with real Bitcoin mechanics in mind. |
| 5 | How Bitcoin Blocks and Confirmations Work | Understand Bitcoin block production, confirmation logic, and network consensus and how to reflect this transparently in UX. |
| 6 | What a Bitcoin Wallet Actually Does | Clarify the real role of wallets from key management to transaction construction to user safety beyond just showing balances. |
| 7 | Security Model of Bitcoin Wallets | Study real-world Bitcoin wallet threats from device loss to theft, and how to design security flows that protect users under pressure. |
| 8 | Wallet Product Architecture Mapping | Map out custodial and non-custodial wallet architectures, infrastructure needs, risk points, and scaling challenges. |
| 9 | Designing a Bitcoin Wallet for Real Users | Translate Bitcoin’s complexity into intuitive, human-first UX: for first-time users, refugees, merchants, and everyday savers. |
| 10 | Designing Bitcoin Support for Existing Custodial Wallets | How to integrate Bitcoin into multi-asset custodial wallets practically, respecting Bitcoin’s special characteristics without overwhelming users. |
| 11 | Bitcoin Address Types, Multi-Address Watching, Batching, and Webhooks | Master the operational basics of Bitcoin address formats, address monitoring, transaction batching, and webhook-driven systems. |
| 12 | Designing Bitcoin Payment Products for Merchants | Blueprint for creating Bitcoin payment acceptance products: onboarding merchants simply, safely, and with strong incentives. |
| 13 | Bitcoin Liquidity and Treasury Management for Wallets | Learn how to manage Bitcoin inflows, outflows, treasury security, and liquidity risks at platform and operational scale. |
| 14 | Practical Bitcoin Product and UX Guidelines for Designers and Product Managers | Real-world design and product guidelines for building Bitcoin apps, services, and integrations users can trust and survive on. |
| 15 | Bitcoin Security Operations (SecOps) for Companies | How to run Bitcoin security systems properly: treasury protection, multi-sig cold storage, hot wallet limits, incident response. |
| 16 | Bitcoin Product Revenue Models: Building Sustainable Businesses | How serious Bitcoin products generate revenue honestly spreads, fees, premium services without betraying user trust. |
| 17 | Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) for Bitcoin Products | How to define, track, and optimize Bitcoin-specific metrics like transaction trust, liquidity efficiency, retention, and growth health. |
| 18 | Marketing Bitcoin Products | How to market Bitcoin products based on real problem-solving, survival use cases, storytelling, and circular economy activation. |
| 19 | Building Bitcoin Products for Activists, Refugees, and Displaced Persons | How to design Bitcoin products for survival: fear, flight, censorship, hunger designing dignity into every pixel and flow. |
| 20 | How Companies Can Incorporate Bitcoin Responsibly and Sustainably | A guide for any fintech, payment platform, or financial app adding Bitcoin wisely balancing user freedom, revenue, and trust. |
| 21 | Mastering Bitcoin Product Building: Final Reflections and Action Tasks | Final practical exercises, checklists, and mastery reflections to turn knowledge into durable Bitcoin product discipline for life. |
